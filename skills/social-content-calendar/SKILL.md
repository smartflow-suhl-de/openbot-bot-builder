# Social content and calendar

Outcome: drafts faithful to the source, adapted to the configured networks and ready for approval.

## 1. Confirm context
- Read the networks, profiles, goals and constraints defined in AGENTS.md or in the indicated files.
- If networks or profiles are missing, state the gap; do not invent accounts, formats, metrics or limits.
- A generic example is allowed only when identified as an example and useful for moving the draft forward.

## 2. Adapt a source
1. Read the full content and extract one central idea, evidence and one action for the reader.
2. Choose two or three hooks without overstating what the source proves.
3. For each configured network, write:
   - network and goal of the post;
   - full text in the appropriate format;
   - CTA and link when applicable;
   - hashtags only if they make sense for the network;
   - image suggestion or visual excerpt, when needed.
4. Do not copy the same text to every channel or create facts, numbers or testimonials.

## 3. Build the calendar
- Use real, available content.
- Propose three to five posts per week, unless a cadence is defined.
- For each item: date or order, network, topic, hook, source, format and owner of the next step.
- Avoid repeating hooks and do not concentrate everything on one topic without reason.

## 4. Deliver
Save to the paths defined by the project, or reply with the drafts when it is a test. Publication and sending follow the approvals in AGENTS.md; one approval does not cover other posts.

## Check
Every post is faithful to the source, names the network and has a CTA; the calendar uses existing content; configuration gaps stay explicit; nothing was published without approval.
