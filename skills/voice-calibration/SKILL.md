# Voice calibration

Outcome: an evidence-based voice guide and new drafts comparable to the samples. Use the guide and sample locations defined in AGENTS.md.

## 1. Gather evidence
- Request 3–5 texts written by the user when there are no samples; prefer the channel of the task.
- Separate samples by channel and identify which ones were edited by someone else.
- Without samples, do not attribute characteristics to the user. If a provisional editorial draft is useful, label it explicitly as such.

## 2. Analyze without copying content
For each sample, observe:
- first person, formality, humor, emotion and relationship with the reader;
- sentence and paragraph length and rhythm;
- opening, transitions, conclusion and calls to action;
- recurring vocabulary, avoided expressions and use of lists, numbers and questions;
- level of detail, examples, certainties and qualifications.

Distinguish a repeated pattern from an isolated occurrence. Do not infer personality or opinions beyond the text.

## 3. Build the guide
Create or update the guide with:
1. a summary of the voice in up to five traits;
2. rules per channel;
3. preferred vocabulary and terms to avoid;
4. rhythm, structure and formatting;
5. three short examples taken from the samples, when authorized;
6. decisions still open and what remains to be observed.

Cite the sample used as evidence for each trait. When you receive an edit, record the difference and the known reason, without inventing an explanation.

## 4. Write and compare
1. Confirm channel, audience, goal and length.
2. Read the guide and one or two samples from the same channel.
3. Write the draft without reusing sample sentences out of context.
4. Review once for voice and once for clarity.
5. Compare opening, rhythm, vocabulary and conclusion with the samples; correct only concrete divergences.

## Check
- Every trait in the guide has evidence.
- The text preserves the intent and does not sound like a generic imitation.
- The user can edit the guide and understand where each rule came from.
- Without enough samples, the limitation stays explicit.
